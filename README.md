# Ryan Moore
is an accomplished generalist with 15+ years of experience in market-facing roles and internal management consulting, including more than a decade in health care. He currently serves as a Large Group Account Manager at Kaiser Permanente in Honolulu.

Earlier in his career, Ryan led clinical marketing programs at Hill-Rom (a Baxter company), drove international market expansion and business development at Young Innovations, and built go-to-market strategies for companies in several industries including specialty coffee, fintech and corporate housing. His work has consistently centered on relationship building, translating market intelligence into strategy, and driving growth through cross-functional collaboration.

Ryan holds a B.A. in Business/Economics from Wheaton College and is currently an Executive MBA candidate at the University of Hawaii at Manoa's Shidler College of Business. He has been active in volunteer and community leadership efforts for nearly three decades in the cities he has called home, including Detroit, Chicago, Los Angeles, San Francisco and Honolulu.
