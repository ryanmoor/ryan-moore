# Kill Zone, Figure 1: VC deals and investment in the social media space

## Provenance

- **Source:** Kamepalli, S. K., Rajan, R., & Zingales, L. (2020, revised June
  2022). *Kill Zone* (NBER Working Paper No. 27146). National Bureau of
  Economic Research. Figure 1, "VC Investment and Deals in the Social Media
  Space," printed p. 34 (PDF p. 35). Underlying data: PitchBook.
- **How the values were obtained:** the paper prints no numbers for Figure 1.
  Each value was read off the figure's two embedded raster images (200 dpi)
  at that year's gridline, by script, on 2026-10-11. They are approximations
  of the plotted lines, not PitchBook data.
- **Precision:** about ±10 deals and ±$15M. Values marked * (2001 and 2019) are
  extrapolated from the line's slope just inside the chart frame, where the
  line meets the frame and tick marks; their error is about ±25 deals and
  ±$40M.
- **Checked:** against the figure on PDF p. 35, 2026-10-11.

## Definitions (from the figure's caption)

- `DEALS`: number of early-stage start-ups financed by a venture capitalist in
  the social media space, per year.
- `INVEST_USD_M`: dollar amount of funding going to early-stage financings of
  start-ups in the social media space, per year, in millions of USD (nominal;
  the paper does not say it is inflation-adjusted).
